Eligibility Criteria to Become an LIC Agent

Before applying, ensure you meet the basic requirements to start your LIC journey.

Checklist:

  • Must have passed 10th standard (minimum qualification)
  • Must be 18 years or older
  • Must clear a basic interview process
  • Must complete IRDAI-mandated training
  • Must pass the IC38 Examination
2

Application Process for LIC Agency

To begin your LIC agent journey, you need to submit an application.

  • Collect the form from your nearest LIC branch ordownload it from here.
  • Submit the filled form to LIC or through a support team.

3

Required Documents

Prepare the following documents for your LIC agent application:

  • 10th Marksheet (mandatory)
  • Highest Qualification Certificate
  • PAN Card
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Address Proof
  • 6 Passport Size Photographs
  • Cancelled Cheque
4

Application Fees Breakdown (₹850)

The total application fee is ₹850, which covers training and examination costs.

Component Amount
Registration Fee ₹150
Training Fee ₹150
Examination Fee ₹550
Total ₹850
👉 This fee is charged to facilitate your training and exam process.
5

IC38 Training & Examination

After application approval, you must complete mandatory training and pass the exam.

Training:

  • Conducted by IRDAI
  • Duration: 25 hours
  • Available in online & offline modes
  • Certificate provided after completion

Examination:

  • Based on IC38 training syllabus
  • Mandatory to pass for license approval
6

Licensing & Final Step

After successfully clearing the IC 38 LIC agent examination, you will get an official license from LIC to start working as a LIC Agent in India.

You’ll be awarded an appointment letter from IRDAI.

If You Fail:

  • Reattempt allowed with ₹500 fee
  • No limit on attempts
