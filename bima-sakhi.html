What is Bima Sakhi?

Bima Sakhi is an initiative by Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) to empower women as insurance advisors in their communities. It helps them achieve financial independence while guiding families toward a secure financial future.

Whether you are a homemaker, an unemployed woman, or someone seeking a flexible career - Bima Sakhi opens the door to professional growth with zero prior experience needed.

Enrollment Process

Simple 6-step process to begin your journey as a Bima Sakhi advisor.

01

Visit LIC Office

Visit your nearest LIC branch or contact a Development Officer to begin enrollment.

02

Register on Portal

Register on LIC/IRDAI portal. A unique reference number will be generated for you.

03

Complete IC-38 Training

Attend the free 25-hour IC-38 training programme approved by IRDAI.

04

Pass Online Exam

Clear the online MCQ exam on NSE-IT platform. Minimum score: 15 out of 50.

05

Get Appointment Letter

Receive your official appointment letter and agency number from LIC.

06

Start Earning

Begin working as a Bima Sakhi - sell policies, earn stipend and commissions!

Yes, and this is one of the biggest advantages. Your stipend is just the guaranteed base. Every policy you sell earns you a commission on top of it. There is absolutely no cap — the more you sell, the more you earn. Many Bima Sakhis earn well beyond the stipend amount through commissions alone.
