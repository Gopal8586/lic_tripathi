What is LIC Agency? +
LIC agency is a career opportunity where you work with Life Insurance Corporation of India as a financial advisor. You help people secure their future through insurance and earn commission-based income.
Who can become an LIC agent? +
Anyone who:
  • Is 18 years or above
  • Has minimum 10th pass (rural) / 12th pass (urban)
  • Has good communication skills
can apply.
Is this a job or business? +
LIC agency is a self-employed business opportunity, not a salaried job. You earn based on your performance.
What is the earning potential? +
There is no income limit. Your earnings include:
  • First-year commission
  • Renewal commission (passive income)
  • Bonus & incentives
Top agents earn ₹50,000 to ₹2 lakh+ per month depending on performance.
Do I need prior experience? +
No experience is required. LIC provides:
  • Complete training
  • Product knowledge
  • Sales guidance
What is the training process? +
You need to complete:
  • 25 hours training
  • Pass IRDAI exam conducted by Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India
Is there any investment required? +
Very minimal:
  • Training fees (approx ₹500–₹1000)
  • Exam fees
No heavy investment needed.
Can I do LIC agency part-time? +
Yes, LIC agency can be done:
  • Full-time
  • Part-time (along with job/business)
What support will I get? +
You will get:
  • Daily guidance
  • Marketing support
  • Lead generation ideas
  • Personal mentorship
How soon can I start earning? +
You can start earning immediately after getting your license and closing your first policy.
What are the growth opportunities? +
You can grow to:
  • Senior Agent
  • Club Member (MDRT, etc.)
  • Development Officer (promotion path)
Why choose LIC? +
  • Trusted brand of India
  • High claim settlement ratio
  • Strong product portfolio
  • Long-term income security
Is this suitable for women? +
Absolutely! LIC agency is one of the best careers for:
  • Housewives
  • Working women
  • Entrepreneurs
Flexible timings + good income potential.
How do I apply? +
You can apply by:
  • Contacting a recruitment officer
  • Filling a form
  • Attending a short interview
